# Slides

Readable on a projector, a video call and a phone. Priority: clarity > readability > hierarchy > simplicity.

- Each slide is a top-level frame 1920 × 1080, placed in a row with 100 px between slides. Keep content ≥ 100 px from the edges.
- One idea per slide. The title states the takeaway ("Revenue doubled in Q3"), not the topic ("Revenue").
- If content doesn't fit at the required sizes, split the slide or cut content. Never shrink fonts.
- Type: max two families; titles ≥ 56 px, body ≥ 28 px; key numbers can be huge (160+). Line height 1.1–1.2. Weight, not many sizes, creates hierarchy. No ALL CAPS except small labels.
- Short phrases, no paragraphs, max ~6 bullets.
- Color: 2–3 core colors plus neutrals; accent only for emphasis; text always high-contrast.
- Data: one insight per chart, highlight the key datapoint, remove chart junk.
- Consistency over creativity: same title position, margins and grid on every slide. Brand guidelines are adapted for slides (bigger, more space), never followed at the cost of readability.

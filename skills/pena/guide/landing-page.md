# Landing pages

## Purpose
- One conversion goal (sign up, buy, book a demo). Every section moves the visitor toward it; cut what doesn't.
- Sell the outcome, not the tool: show who the visitor becomes.

## Content
- The hero is the whole pitch compressed: what it is, why it matters, what to do next. One headline, one call to action.
- Headline strength, best to worst: transformation ("Finally feel in control") > outcome ("Ship in days") > benefit ("10x faster") > feature ("AI assistant").
- Imagery shows people/situations in the outcome state. Text over images always gets a contrast treatment (overlay gradient or its own container).
- The CTA is the most prominent element. Keep the accent color for actions only.

## Structure
- Desktop frame 1440 wide, `layout: "vertical"`, height `fit_content`. Sections are full-width frames with horizontal padding ~120.
- One alignment axis per section — don't mix centered and left-aligned blocks inside it.
- Alternate dense text sections with visual ones; use a dark section for credibility (logos, testimonials, numbers).
- Narrative: promise → how it works → proof → objections/FAQ → final CTA → footer.
- Type: body ≥ 16 px (18 preferred), clear jumps between levels (e.g. 64 / 40 / 24 / 18). Center at most 2–3 lines. Line length 50–75 characters (`fixed-width` text, width ~640).
- Write the copy first, then design around it.
